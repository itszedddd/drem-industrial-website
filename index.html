About Us

Our Company

Dedicated to delivering high-grade industrial products, engineering solutions, and reliable client service across the Philippines for over 40 years.

DREM Industrial Sales, Inc. is a trusted partner for industrial products and engineering services in the Philippines. With over 40 years of continuous industry experience, we supply certified maintenance chemicals, high-temperature refractories, firefighting systems, and provide reliable civil construction and government permits facilitation. We work closely with our clients to maintain operational excellence, plant safety, and regulatory compliance.

Our Mission

To provide the highest quality industrial products and services at competitive prices, while building lasting relationships with our clients through trust, reliability, and exceptional customer support.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred industrial supplier in the Philippines, recognized for our commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainable business practices that drive industry growth.

Our Services

Professional civil engineering, construction works, and regulatory compliance facilitation.

Construction

Civil Works

  • Construction & Structural Works

    Comprehensive civil works and construction services for industrial plants, manufacturing facilities, commercial structures, concrete machinery foundations, and structural retrofitting.

Regulatory Compliance

Permits and Licenses Facilitation

  • Facilitation of New DOLE Permit-to-Operate (PTO)

    Complete documentation, technical evaluation, and processing for new DOLE permits covering mechanical installations and plant machinery.

  • Facilitation of Renewal DOLE PTO

    Timely renewal filing and compliance coordination to maintain uninterrupted operations and government safety approval.

  • Facilitation of Renewal DENR PTO

    End-to-end facilitation of DENR environmental permits to operate, ensuring strict adherence to environmental regulations.

  • DENR Emission Testing

    Certified emission testing of industrial boilers, generator sets (gensets), and ambient air quality testing per DENR standards.
